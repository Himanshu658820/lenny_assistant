**The Retention Problem in Consumer Products**
=====================================================

**Hook**
--------

Did you know that 75% of AI-powered consumer products fail to meet their retention goals? (Source: [Beyond Vibe Checks: A PM's Complete Guide to Evals](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/beyond-vibe-checks-a-pms-complete)) This staggering statistic highlights the need for a more effective approach to product development and retention.

**Problem**
---------

Retention is a major challenge for consumer products, especially those powered by AI. Despite the hype surrounding AI, many products struggle to keep users engaged over time. This is often due to a lack of understanding of user needs and behaviors, as well as inadequate product design and development processes. Product teams often focus on building features and functionality, but neglect to consider the user experience and how it will evolve over time.

**Tension**
---------

To improve retention, product teams need to adopt a more iterative and user-centered approach to product development. This involves continuously testing and refining the product to ensure it meets user needs and expectations. However, this requires a deep understanding of user behavior and a willingness to iterate and adapt. Product teams must balance the need for innovation and experimentation with the need for stability and reliability.

**Insight**
---------

One key insight is that AI-powered products require a different approach to testing and iteration. Traditional testing methods, such as A/B testing, are not sufficient for AI-powered products. Instead, product teams need to use evaluation (eval) agents to test and refine the product in real-time. Eval agents can simulate user interactions and provide feedback on the product's performance, allowing product teams to identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions.

**Application**
--------------

So, how can product teams apply this insight to improve retention? Here are some key strategies:

* **Refine eval prompts**: Continuously adjust your eval prompts based on results until performance meets your standards. (Source: [Beyond Vibe Checks: A PM's Complete Guide to Evals](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/beyond-vibe-checks-a-pms-complete)) This involves regularly reviewing and refining the eval prompts to ensure they are effective in simulating user interactions and providing accurate feedback.
* **Expand your dataset**: Regularly add new examples and edge cases to test whether your eval prompts can generalize effectively. This involves continuously collecting and incorporating new data into the eval process to ensure that the product is performing well across a range of scenarios.
* **Iterate on your agent prompt**: Use eval agents to test and refine the product in real-time, and iterate on the agent prompt to improve performance. This involves using the feedback from the eval agents to refine the product and improve its performance over time.

**Reflection**
-------------

Another key strategy is to use reflection to get out of a loop. Rather than asking the AI to fix syntax errors, ask it to come up with a list of possible reasons an error exists. This can help product teams identify and address issues more effectively. By using reflection, product teams can gain a deeper understanding of the product's behavior and identify areas for improvement.

**Fine-tuning**
-------------

Fine-tuning is another important technique for improving retention. By fine-tuning a model on specific data, product teams can tailor the product to meet user needs and expectations. Fine-tuning involves adjusting the model's internal weights to specialize its responses for a specific use case, while preserving the general knowledge it learned during pre-training. This can help product teams create more effective and engaging products that meet user needs.

**Local Developer Assistants**
-----------------------------

Finally, product teams can use local developer assistants, such as GitHub Copilot and Cursor, to improve retention. These tools can help product teams resolve bugs and troubleshoot issues more effectively, reducing the time and effort required to maintain the product. By using local developer assistants, product teams can focus on building and improving the product, rather than spending time on maintenance and debugging.

**The Takeaway**
----------------

To improve retention in consumer products, product teams need to adopt a more iterative and user-centered approach to product development. This involves using eval agents to test and refine the product in real-time, refining eval prompts, expanding the dataset, iterating on the agent prompt, using reflection to get out of a loop, fine-tuning models, and using local developer assistants. By applying these strategies, product teams can improve retention and create more successful AI-powered consumer products.
