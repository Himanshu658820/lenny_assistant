Retention Tactics

Key Takeaways

Mastering evals is crucial for building AI products. It's an emerging skill that can make or break a product.

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is a technique that gives models access to additional information at run-time. It's like giving the model an open-book test instead of having it answer from memory.

When you don't give the model the context it needs to answer your question through RAG, it can lead to "hallucinations".

Retention Strategies

  • Be hyperspecific when describing changes for subsequent prompts.
  • Use clear and concise language to avoid confusion.
  • Provide context to the model to ensure accurate answers.

Best Practices

  • Use RAG to provide context to the model.
  • Be specific and clear in your prompts.
  • Provide feedback to the model to improve its performance.

Remember, mastering evals is crucial for building successful AI products.
